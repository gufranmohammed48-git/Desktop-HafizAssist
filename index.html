Your recitation companion

Find your recitation rhythm.

Two ways to follow your recitation. Choose the mode that suits your practice.

Easy DEFAULT

Keep your focus on the flow.

  • Forgiving recognition. Exact words and small spelling or sound differences pass green.
  • Skipped words turn red. When the following words confirm your position, the tracker marks the missing words and continues.
  • Different recitation pauses tracking. Three confirmed unmatched words at the same position turn the current word red. Correct it to continue.

Strict

Notice the words that need attention.

  • Exact matches pass green. Close words with one- or two-letter differences can pass yellow as unclear. Very different short words are not close matches.
  • Skipped words turn red. The tracker continues once the following words confirm where you are.
  • Different recitation pauses tracking. A confirmed mismatch with no matching nearby continuation turns the current word red. Correct it to continue.
Green · accepted Yellow · unclear Red · error history

How skipping works. Both modes can recover past up to two words. Skipping one needs two following exact matches; skipping two needs three. The limit is two skipped words per recognition result.

Your corrections count. Correct the word and keep reciting. It stays red as a reminder of the mistake, and the error is counted only once.

Switch anytime. Your mode applies to new recognition updates and stays selected across chapter and Quran-page changes. Reloading starts in Easy.

Colors reflect what the app hears. Speech recognition can mishear you, so these marks are a guide for practice, not a full assessment of pronunciation or tajweed.
Made for your Quran journey

A better way to practice

We have updated Quran recitation tracker to make your practice smoother and easier to follow.

  • Smoother tracking: improved word-by-word movement as you recite.
  • Easy & Strict: skipped words stay red. Reading a different verse pauses tracking until you get back on track. Strict also shows unclear words in yellow.
  • A refreshed Mushaf: Single or Double view, with colors that follow your theme.
